## Patterns

- **Test-set selection is the whole game.** The same model can look 2–3x better or worse depending on which benchmark the number is drawn from: MAI-Transcribe-2 is "5.2% avg WER" on Microsoft's own 60-language FLEURS mix but "2.0%" on Artificial Analysis's English-weighted AA-WER; ElevenLabs Scribe v2 is "2.2%" on AA-WER but "7.5%" macro-averaged across Superwhisper's 8-dataset competitor benchmark. No vendor and no independent leaderboard agree on one shared test set, so cross-vendor ranking only holds within a single benchmark, never across them.
- **Stale vendor numbers get cited as if current.** Speechmatics' only locatable accuracy claims ("25% advantage vs Whisper," named competitor numbers for AWS/Azure/Google) are dated March–May 2023 — three and a half years old — with no "Ursa 2" page found anywhere. The current independent contrast (AA-WER 4.0% for "Speechmatics Enhanced") already shows real improvement since 2023; a separate, differently-constructed independent benchmark (Pipecat, voice-agent-style short utterances) puts Speechmatics even lower (1.07% pooled WER) — a different test set, not a direct multiple of the 2023 number, but still evidence the vendor hasn't refreshed its own public claim to match.
- **Several big-name 2026 launches publish zero numbers.** Google (Chirp 2, Chirp 3), Azure AI Speech (base tier), AWS Transcribe, and Deepgram Flux all have confirmed "no published claim" or "page not retrieved" — the three hyperscalers conspicuously never publish a WER figure at all, while every specialized/challenger vendor (ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI, Deepgram Nova, Speechmatics, Soniox, Gladia, Mistral, Microsoft MAI) leads with one.
- **"Beats Whisper" is the one head-to-head claim nearly everyone makes, but by wildly different margins** — AssemblyAI (30% lower hallucination rate), Speechmatics (25% relative WER advantage, 2023), Rev.ai Reverb (beats Whisper large-v3 by 4–9 points on three test sets), Mistral (beats GPT-4o-mini-transcribe/Gemini/AssemblyAI/Deepgram on FLEURS), Wispr Flow Canto (beats OpenAI/AssemblyAI/Deepgram on a proprietary challenge set). Few name the *same* competitor on the *same* test set, so none of these claims are directly comparable to each other.
- **Products built on proprietary models (Wispr Flow Canto, Otter.ai, Aqua Voice Avalon) market accuracy as their core differentiator and publish specific comparative claims; products that wrap or don't disclose their STT vendor (Superwhisper's own product, Granola, tl;dv per the existing notes) publish none** — accuracy marketing appears to track directly with who actually owns the acoustic model, not who ships the nicest app.
